## 1) Who to target first — and is your guess right?

**Your guess is directionally right, but too broad.**  
“Self-led lifters who already track workouts and macros” is a good beachhead, but it describes a *behavior*, not a buying trigger. Many of these people already tolerate two apps, have optimized their workflow, and won’t switch just for “one app.”

**Target this narrower segment first:**

> **Plateaued dual-trackers:** self-coached intermediate lifters in the US/Canada who already log meals and workouts in separate apps, are in a cut / lean bulk / recomp, and are unsure whether to adjust calories, cardio, training volume, or recovery.

Best-fit profile:
- 24–40, lifting 6 months–5 years, 3–5 sessions/week
- Tracks food and lifts consistently, usually in two separate apps
- Has hit a plateau or recently asked, “Should I change my calories or my training?”
- Has tried to reconcile both logs manually — spreadsheet, notes, Reddit post, or pasting logs into ChatGPT
- Pays for at least one fitness app or has paid for a coach/template before
- **Exclude for now:** true beginners, casual trackers, advanced lifters with human coaches, and people who love their current stack.

**Why this group:** they already track, understand data, feel the pain of guessing, and may pay for a decision — not another logger. Your paid product should be positioned as a **weekly AI check-in / next adjustment**, not just “AI coach.”

Your real wedge is not “one app instead of two.” It’s:

> “You already have the data. You don’t know what to change next. We turn your meal + workout logs into one clear adjustment — and you stay in control.”

## 2) What to say

### Recommended positioning

**For self-coached lifters who already track meals and workouts:**  
[App] puts both logs in one place and gives you one AI check-in that reads them together — so you know whether to adjust calories, training volume, or recovery. You approve or dismiss each suggestion.

### Headline options to test

**Recommended lead:**
> **One app for your meals and lifts. An AI coach that reads both — and gives you one next adjustment.**

Subhead:
> For lifters who already track both but are tired of juggling two apps and guessing what to change.

Other variants:

1. **Pain / switching**
> Still logging meals in one app and lifts in another? Get one AI check-in based on both — so you stop guessing what to change.

2. **Plateau / outcome**
> Stalled in your cut or bulk? Your meal and workout logs can tell you whether to change calories, volume, or recovery. One suggestion. You decide.

3. **ChatGPT replacement**
> Stop pasting your logs into ChatGPT. Get an AI coach that already sees your meals and workouts.

4. **Control / trust**
> AI suggests. You approve or dismiss. Free logging, paid coaching.

### What not to lead with

- “Nutrition and training should work together” — true, but abstract. It doesn’t create urgency or tell someone why to switch now.
- “All-in-one app” — weak by itself. Many people tolerate two apps.
- “AI coach” alone — crowded and vague. Say what the AI does: **reads both logs and suggests one next adjustment.**

## 3) What to try this week

**Goal:** validate the target and message, not scale. Use your existing app. Don’t build new features this week.

### Success criteria for the week

Aim for:
- 10 qualified conversations with dual-trackers
- At least 6 confirm they currently use two apps
- At least 4 mention plateau / uncertainty about what to change
- At least 3 install and log both a meal and a workout within 48 hours
- At least 2 show strong willingness to pay or actually commit to a founding plan
- One headline/message clearly outperforms the others

### Day-by-day plan

**Monday — build the test assets**
- Make a simple landing page with 3 headline variants.
- CTA: “Start free” or “Join founding pilot.”
- Add 3 screening questions:  
  1. Do you currently track both meals and workouts?  
  2. Which apps do you use?  
  3. What’s your current goal and have you hit a plateau?
- Build a list of 50 people from Reddit, Discord, Instagram/TikTok comments, and fitness communities who mention tracking both, plateaus, or pasting logs into ChatGPT.

**Tuesday — recruit**
DM 30 qualified people. Use something like:

> Hey — I’m researching for an app built for lifters who track both macros and workouts. You mentioned [app] + [app]. Could I ask 3 quick questions or do a 10-min call? Not selling — I’ll share what I learn.

Also post a non-spammy feedback request in 2–3 communities where allowed:
> I’m researching how lifters connect macro data and workout data. If you use two apps, what’s your current process? I’ll share what I learn.

**Wednesday–Friday — run 8–10 interviews**

Ask about past behavior, not opinions:

- Walk me through the last time you logged a meal and a workout. Which apps? What did you do with that data?
- Last time you changed calories or training volume, how did you decide?
- Have you ever tried to combine or interpret both logs? Spreadsheet, notes, ChatGPT, coach?
- What do you pay for now? What would make you switch?
- If you got one weekly adjustment based on both logs, what would make you trust it? What would make you ignore it?
- After questions only: show the product. Ask: “Who is this not for? Would you pay $10/month for the AI check-in? Would you start now?”

Do **not** ask “Do you like this idea?” Ask what they already do and what they already pay for.

**Thursday — offer 10 founding accounts**
Personally onboard 10 users. Offer to set them up and review their first AI suggestions in exchange for feedback. Track:
- Did they log a meal and workout within 48 hours?
- Did they understand the first AI suggestion?
- Did they approve/dismiss it? Why?
- Did they say they’d pay? Did anyone actually pay?

**Saturday — optional ad test**
If you have $100–200, run a small US/Canada ad test on Reddit or Meta with 3 message variants. Send traffic to the landing page. Measure qualified signups and CTA clicks, not just impressions.

**Sunday — decide**
Pick:
- One target: plateaued dual-trackers
- One message: the headline/variant with the best qualified action
- One objection to address next week — likely trust, data import, or price

### Minimum version if you’re short on time

Do just three things:
1. DM 20 dual-trackers.
2. Do 5 interviews.
3. Put up one landing page with 3 headlines and measure which one gets the most qualified CTA clicks.

The core bet to test this week: **Do plateaued lifters who already track both logs care enough about “one AI check-in that tells me what to change” to switch — not just about having one app?**
