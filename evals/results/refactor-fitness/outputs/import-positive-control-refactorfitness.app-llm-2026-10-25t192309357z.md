# Refactor Fitness

> Refactor Fitness is a nutrition and strength app for people who want to keep their strength while they lose weight, including people on GLP-1 medications. One app for every meal, and every set.

Official site: https://refactorfitness.app/

## Best For

- Self-led lifters who track meals, workouts, PRs, body measurements, and progress.
- People getting serious about strength training who want help with the details of nutrition, lifting, and progress.
- Users who want macro tracking, workout tracking, and AI coaching recommendations that consider meals, workouts, PRs, body measurements, and progress together.
- People focused on body recomposition, cutting, bulking, strength progress, or consistency.
- Men over 40 (roughly 38-55) rebuilding strength and energy around a career, a family, and a home gym, with workouts generated from their own equipment and available time.

## Not Primarily Built For

- Running plans or cardio endurance coaching.
- CrossFit-style class programming.
- HYROX-specific guided programs.
- Medical advice, clinical diet support, or guaranteed health outcomes.
- Human coaching services.
- A chatbot-only fitness setup.

## Product Summary

Refactor Fitness connects what you eat with how you train. Users can track calories, macros (protein, carbs, and fat), fiber, meals, workouts, PRs, body measurements, and progress in one app. Spot AI analyzes that full picture and recommends what to adjust while keeping the user in control.

Refactor helps with the planning, tracking, and review details so users can focus effort on lifting, eating, and sleeping.

Refactor Fitness does not ask for doses and does not provide injection reminders, side-effect tracking, or medication advice. Timeline events are optional, display-only chart markers: free to create, shown on Progress charts with Pro, and never sent to Spot or any AI.

## Shipped Capabilities

- Nutrition tracking for calories, macros, and fiber.
- Meal tracking by search, barcode, saved meal, photo scan, and plain text.
- Workout tracking with sets, reps, weight, cardio, programs, and templates.
- Spot AI can generate structured workout programs.
- Progress tracking, analytics, and body measurements.
- Spot AI coaching recommendations that consider meals, workouts, PRs, body measurements, and progress together.
- Daily AI reports.
- User review before AI meal estimates are saved.
- User control over Spot recommendations.
- Exercise snacks: optional, short movement breaks during desk hours, free for everyone, tracked separately from workouts.

## Pricing And Availability

- Available in: United States and Canada.
- Distribution: Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
- Public APK downloads: not available to customers.
- Free tier: unlimited meal and workout tracking, with 3 workout templates and a 90-day history view. Older logs stay saved and sync normally.
- Refactor Pro: Spot AI coaching, unlimited history, unlimited workout templates, Progress Tab features, daily AI reports, and 300 AI messages per week.
- Pro Monthly: $14.99/month.
- Pro Annual: $119.99/year.
- Free trial: new accounts get 14 days of Pro free, with no card and no automatic charge.

## Safety And Claim Boundaries

Refactor Fitness is not a medical service, human coaching service, licensed trainer, dietitian, or guaranteed-results program. Spot AI provides software-generated recommendations that users can accept, edit, or ignore. Refactor Fitness should not be described as prescribing diets, treating conditions, curing conditions, or guaranteeing weight loss, muscle gain, or body recomposition. Not affiliated with any drug manufacturer. Not a medical device.
