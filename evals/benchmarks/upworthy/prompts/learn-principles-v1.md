# learn-principles-v1 (pipeline tuning on the TRAIN split only)

Below are real headline A/B tests from a viral-news publisher. Each test lists the headlines tested for one article, ranked by real click-through rate; the first one won by a clear margin.

Study them and write the principles that best predict which headline wins *within the same article*. Rules:
- 8–12 principles, each one sentence plus a short "e.g." contrast written in your own words (not copied from a test).
- Each must be general (about wording, structure, framing or emotion), not about a topic, person or event.
- Order them by how often they decided a test, and say when a principle does NOT hold (common exceptions).
- Finish with a 3-step procedure for comparing headlines with the principles.

Write plain Markdown only.
